ThreadLibrary Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

ThreadLibrary is an extension for Google Chrome that helps you organize, search, and save your conversations from claude.ai. It is developed by Flamur Haxholli ("we"). This policy explains what information ThreadLibrary handles, where it is kept, and what you can do with it. It applies to the current version of ThreadLibrary, which is free.

The short version

  • Everything stays on your computer. ThreadLibrary keeps your library in your browser's own storage, on this device.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere. ThreadLibrary has no servers and makes no internet connections. It uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • We never see your information, so we can't sell, share, or rent it.
  • You stay in control. You can export or delete your data at any time.

What information ThreadLibrary handles

  • Conversations you import. When you choose a Claude data export on the Import screen, ThreadLibrary reads the conversations in it: titles, messages, and dates. From a ZIP file it opens only the conversation files; it never opens the account, project, or memory files that Claude's export also contains.
  • The Claude conversation you are viewing. While the ThreadLibrary side panel is open and your current browser tab shows a conversation on claude.ai, ThreadLibrary reads that page's title and the messages shown on it, so it can tell you whether the conversation is already in your library. It keeps them only if you choose to save the conversation or one of its answers.
  • What you create in ThreadLibrary: folders, tags, notes, saved answers, clips, categories, and settings.
  • Backups you restore. When you choose a ThreadLibrary backup file, ThreadLibrary reads it to restore your library.

ThreadLibrary does not read other websites. On claude.ai it does not read your account details, cookies, or passwords.

Where your information is stored

Your library is stored in your browser's storage on this device (a browser database called IndexedDB), in an area that only ThreadLibrary can open. It is not copied to any server and is not synced to your other devices.

ThreadLibrary does not add its own encryption. Your library is protected the same way as the rest of your browser data: by your computer account and your Chrome profile. Anyone who can use your Chrome profile on this computer could see it.

What ThreadLibrary does not do

  • It does not send your conversations or any other information to us, to Anthropic, to AI services, or to anyone else.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, cookies for tracking, or any other tracking.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share information. We never receive it.
  • It does not download or run code from the internet. Everything it runs is included in the extension.

Some buttons open pages for you, such as "Open in Claude" or the download links in Claude's export list. These open in a normal browser tab, just as if you had clicked a link yourself; ThreadLibrary does not send anything along with them.

The permissions ThreadLibrary asks for

  • Side panel: to show ThreadLibrary next to the pages you are viewing.
  • Unlimited storage: so Chrome doesn't refuse to store a large library because of its usual storage limit.
  • Access to claude.ai: to recognize which Claude conversation is open and to read it when you ask. Chrome describes this permission as "Read and change your data on claude.ai". ThreadLibrary never changes your conversations. It changes what a Claude page shows only when you ask it to find a message there, with "Show in Claude" or "Load earlier messages": it scrolls the conversation to the message, which makes Claude load older messages just as when you scroll up yourself, and "Show in Claude" outlines the message for a few seconds. It stops when it finds the message, when it has looked through the whole conversation, when Claude stops showing more messages, when you choose Cancel, when you scroll the page yourself, or after 2 minutes at most. If it doesn't find the message, it puts you back where you were. Just reading a page never scrolls it.

Your choices

  • Export: create a backup of your whole library, or export a conversation, a clip, or your saved items, from within ThreadLibrary.
  • Delete: delete individual conversations, folders, tags, saved answers, or clips, or delete everything at once in Settings → Danger zone → Delete all library data. Uninstalling ThreadLibrary also removes its data from Chrome.
  • Deleting something in ThreadLibrary never deletes anything in your Claude account.

Limited use

ThreadLibrary uses the information it handles only to provide its features: organizing, searching, and saving your conversations. The information is not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, credit decisions, or any other purpose, and not read by any person other than you, because it never leaves your device.

Children

ThreadLibrary is not directed at children under 13, and we do not collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of ThreadLibrary handles information differently, for example by connecting to a server, we will update this policy before that version is released and describe the change here. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when the policy last changed.
